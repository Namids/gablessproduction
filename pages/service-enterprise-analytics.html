Enterprise Analytics

Turning Healthcare Data into Actionable Organizational Intelligence

Hospitals generate large volumes of clinical, operational, financial, quality, and patient-experience data. The challenge is turning those data into timely information leaders and clinicians can use.

Enterprise analytics creates a coordinated approach for transforming data into dashboards, measures, insights, and decision-support capabilities.

How GHC Supports You

GHC helps organizations develop analytics strategies connected to real healthcare priorities.

Our services include:

  • Analytics maturity assessment
  • KPI and performance-measure development
  • Executive and clinical dashboards
  • Data-source and integration assessment
  • Quality and operational analytics
  • Cardiovascular service-line analytics
  • Patient-flow and utilization analytics
  • Data governance and standardization
  • Reporting strategy and optimization

We help organizations move from simply collecting data to using information strategically for quality improvement, resource planning, operational management, and better-informed decision-making.
