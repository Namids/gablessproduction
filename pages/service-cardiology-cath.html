Cardiology Cath

Strengthening the Digital and Operational Foundation of the Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory

Cardiac catheterization laboratories are highly coordinated clinical environments where patient safety, imaging, hemodynamic information, procedural documentation, staffing, equipment, and workflow must work together seamlessly.

How GHC Supports You

GHC works with healthcare organizations to strengthen the technology and operational infrastructure supporting cardiac catheterization and interventional cardiology services.

Our consulting support includes:

  • Cath lab workflow assessment and redesign
  • Hemodynamic and cardiovascular information system integration
  • Procedure documentation optimization
  • Imaging and PACS integration
  • EHR interface and interoperability planning
  • Data capture and quality-reporting workflows
  • Clinical and operational readiness assessment
  • Technology implementation and optimization
  • Staff workflow and adoption support
  • Quality, safety, and performance improvement planning

We help organizations create Cath Lab environments in which technology supports, rather than interrupts, clinical care, while promoting efficient workflow, reliable information access, quality improvement, and patient safety.
