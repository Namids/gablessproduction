About Our Services

  • What exactly does Gabless Healthcare Consulting do?

    We help hospitals and healthcare institutions improve how they operate. That covers hospital operations consulting, healthcare technology integration, medical equipment procurement, workflow optimization, IT systems integration and patient care strategy. In practice, most engagements combine several of these rather than just one.

  • Which organisations do you typically work with?

    Our clients are mainly hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and healthcare groups across Nigeria and the wider African region. We work with both private facilities and public institutions, and with organisations at very different stages, from a single growing clinic to multi-site hospital groups.

  • Do you only advise, or do you help with implementation?

    Both. A recommendation nobody can act on isn't worth much, so we stay involved through implementation, whether that means installing and commissioning equipment, integrating systems, or training your staff on new workflows.

  • Can you help us procure medical equipment?

    Yes. We help specify what you actually need, source it through vetted suppliers, and manage delivery, installation and staff training so the equipment is genuinely in service rather than sitting in a store room.

  • Do you offer telemedicine support?

    Yes. We help institutions set up and run telemedicine services, covering the platform itself, the clinical workflow around it, and the staff training needed to make remote consultations work reliably day to day.

Working With Us

  • How do we get started?

    Send us a message through the contact page or call us directly. We'll set up an initial conversation to understand your situation, then follow up with a proposed scope of work and what it would involve. There's no charge for that first conversation.

  • How long does a typical engagement take?

    It depends entirely on scope. A focused assessment might run a few weeks, while a full operational transformation or a multi-site technology rollout can run several months. We agree on timelines with you up front and work in defined phases so you can see progress along the way.

  • How is pricing structured?

    We price per engagement based on scope, duration and the size of your institution, rather than working from a fixed price list. You'll receive a written proposal with clear costs before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

  • How do we make a payment?

    Bank transfer details are on our payment page. If you need an invoice for your finance or procurement team, just ask and we'll issue one.

  • Is our information kept confidential?

    Yes. Healthcare work involves sensitive operational, financial and patient-related information. We treat everything shared with us as confidential and are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before detailed discussions begin.

  • Are you hiring?

    We're always interested in hearing from healthcare professionals, consultants and technology specialists. Our careers page covers what it's like to work here and how to get in touch.
