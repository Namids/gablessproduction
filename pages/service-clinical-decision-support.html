Clinical Decision Support

Delivering Relevant Information at the Point of Decision

Clinical Decision Support brings patient-specific information, clinical knowledge, guidelines, alerts, order sets, reminders, and analytical tools into healthcare workflows to help clinicians make informed decisions.

Effective decision support must be relevant, timely, usable, and appropriately integrated into clinical workflow.

How GHC Supports You

GHC assists organizations in planning and optimizing clinical decision-support capabilities that complement professional clinical judgment.

Our support includes:

  • CDS strategy and readiness assessment
  • Workflow integration
  • Alert and notification optimization
  • Order-set and clinical-content workflow
  • EHR-based decision-support implementation
  • Data and interoperability requirements
  • Governance and clinical oversight
  • Adoption and usability evaluation
  • Performance monitoring and optimization

The GHC Value

Our goal is to help organizations deliver meaningful information to clinicians when it is most useful while reducing unnecessary interruptions and alert burden.
