Cardiology ECG

Making Cardiac Diagnostic Information More Accessible, Connected, and Actionable

Electrocardiography is one of the most widely used tools for evaluating cardiac rhythm and electrical activity. Yet ECG information can become fragmented when devices, interpretation systems, monitoring platforms, and EHRs operate independently.

How GHC Supports You

GHC helps organizations create more connected ECG workflows from acquisition through interpretation, reporting, distribution, and long-term accessibility.

Our support includes:

  • ECG management-system assessment
  • Device and system integration
  • EHR connectivity
  • Workflow and reporting optimization
  • Historical ECG accessibility planning
  • Interface and data validation
  • Implementation support
  • Clinical-user workflow optimization

Our goal is to help make ECG information available to the right authorized clinician at the right point in care while reducing unnecessary manual processes and disconnected data.
