Cardiology Nuclear Medicine

Connecting Advanced Cardiac Imaging with the Wider Clinical Ecosystem

Cardiac nuclear medicine provides physiologic and functional information that can contribute to the evaluation of myocardial perfusion, cardiac function, and selected cardiovascular conditions. Delivering these services efficiently requires coordinated imaging, reporting, scheduling, data management, and integration with broader cardiovascular workflows.

How GHC Supports You

GHC assists organizations with the technology and workflow infrastructure surrounding cardiac nuclear medicine services.

Our consulting support includes:

  • Nuclear Cardiology Workflow Assessment
  • Imaging and PACS connectivity
  • Structured reporting integration
  • EHR interoperability
  • Scheduling and order-management workflow
  • Data accessibility and archiving strategy
  • Technology implementation planning
  • Quality and operational workflow improvement

We help organizations connect cardiac nuclear medicine information to the broader patient record so clinicians can access relevant imaging and reports as part of a more coordinated cardiovascular care experience.
