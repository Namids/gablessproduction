Patient-Centric Solutions

Design Healthcare Around the People It Serves.

Patient-centered care recognizes that quality healthcare must reflect patients' needs, values, preferences, circumstances, and ability to participate in decisions about their care.

Technology and operational improvement should therefore make healthcare easier to navigate—not more complicated.

How GHC Supports You

GHC helps healthcare organizations examine services from the patient’s perspective and design processes that strengthen access, communication, engagement, coordination, and continuity.

Our services include:

  • Patient-journey mapping
  • Access and scheduling improvement
  • Communication workflow assessment
  • Digital patient-engagement strategy
  • Patient portal and digital-access optimization
  • Telehealth workflow design
  • Care-coordination improvement
  • Patient-experience measurement
  • Shared decision-support workflow
  • Health-literacy considerations
  • Equity and accessibility review

Our Goal

To help healthcare organizations create compassionate, coordinated, accessible, and technology-enabled experiences in which patients remain at the center of care.
