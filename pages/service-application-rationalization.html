Application Rationalization

Simplifying the Technology Environment Without Losing Clinical Value

Healthcare organizations often accumulate overlapping, underused, outdated, or disconnected applications over time. This can increase costs, cybersecurity exposure, support complexity, data fragmentation, and clinician frustration.

Application rationalization provides a structured approach for evaluating the technology portfolio and determining which applications should be retained, optimized, consolidated, replaced, integrated, or retired.

How GHC Supports You

GHC works collaboratively with clinical, operational, financial, and IT stakeholders to understand both the technical and real-world clinical value of existing systems.

Our services include:

  • Application inventory and portfolio assessment
  • Clinical and operational stakeholder interviews
  • Redundancy and functionality analysis
  • Workflow-impact assessment
  • Integration and interoperability review
  • Cost and support-burden analysis
  • Risk and dependency assessment
  • Consolidation and retirement roadmap
  • Implementation and change-management planning

We help organizations move toward a simpler, more sustainable digital environment while protecting essential clinical functionality and maintaining continuity of care.
