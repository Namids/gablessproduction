Electronic Health Records

Building a Connected Digital Foundation for Patient Care

Electronic Health Records are central to modern healthcare delivery. When properly designed and implemented, they can support documentation, medication management, ordering, results review, care coordination, communication, quality measurement, and continuity of information across the patient journey.

Poorly designed workflows, however, can create unnecessary burden and inefficiency.
